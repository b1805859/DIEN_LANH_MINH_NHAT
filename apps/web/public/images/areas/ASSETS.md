# Areas image assets

Ten original photo assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool using the supplied 880 × 1788 mockup as a composition reference. No screenshot, screenshot crop, remote random image URL, or equipment photograph is used as an area photograph. The generated scenes are visual interpretations, not documentary photographs of the named places.

The booking photograph was generated separately to match the bare arms, crossed-arm pose, slim figure and uniform of the supplied reference. The existing Services booking photo was initially considered but replaced after visual comparison showed differences in gloves and pose. The original Services asset remains untouched.

Prompts and provenance are recorded in `output/areas-reference/prompt-hero.json`, `prompts-first-four.json`, `prompts-last-four.json`, and `prompt-booking.json`. Phong Điền and Thới Lai were regenerated for closer composition. An additional hero candidate was inspected and rejected because its monument sat lower than the reference; its prompt is recorded in `prompt-hero-v2-discarded.json`.

All selected photographs were visually inspected for composition, coherent roads/buildings, anatomy in the booking band, and absence of watermarks or stray UI/text. WebP conversion uses quality 94. Fine scenery, facial features, exact landmarks, and photographic colors are not identical to the mockup.
